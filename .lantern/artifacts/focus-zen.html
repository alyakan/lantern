What goes away during a Focus

  • The sidebar, the chat list, and other projects (not even searchable)
  • The tab bar (Changes, Files, Activity, Tests, Network, Artifacts): one key opens one
  • Usage meters, badges, the background-task count, the "N asleep" line
  • The topic-slot meter: the limit only speaks up when you try to start one too many
  • Notifications from other projects, and "done" pings from this one
