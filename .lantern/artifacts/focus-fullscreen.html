How it behaves

  • The button: ⤢ in the top bar, next to the Review toggle. Also ⌃⌘F, like macOS full screen. It slides the top bar up and goes true full screen, so the menu bar and Dock go too.
  • Peek: move the mouse to the top edge and the bar slides back down over the content, then away again when you leave. Like Safari and Xcode in full screen.
  • Things that need you still reach you: an approval or question card shows in the chat as today. If one comes from another focus chat, its sidebar dot turns red.
  • Sidebar and the Review pane keep their own toggles (⌘\, the sidebar key), so you can go down to just the chat if you want, but you don't have to.
  • The Desk stays at the top of the sidebar, as the Focus's own Desk: its feed, live lines and needs-you cards only come from the Focus's chats, and the router only picks among them. "How are we on testo?" answers from Lumiform chats only.
  • Ideas through the Desk: a message meant for a project outside the Focus isn't routed or started. The Desk parks it as an idea for that project, with "Not Nahar" and "Start it anyway" (which steps out of the Focus).
  • Focus is the purple pill: the sidebar lists only the Lumiform projects, and the others don't ping. Full screen works with or without a Focus.
