Brainstorm sketch, not a spec

Focus mode

One rule: while a Focus is on, Lantern only has the projects in it. Everything else is asleep, silent, and out of sight on the Mac, the phone and the iPad. Ideas for asleep projects still get caught in two seconds and wait for you, so switching focus never means losing a thought.

It sits on top of Desks, which already exist: a Desk is a named set of projects. A Focus is just "a Desk, locked, on a schedule". So "Lumiform" (ios-app, lumiform-*) during work hours, "Side projects" (Lantern, Nahar, Typewright…) in the evening.

1 · The Mac while Focus is on

Sidebar shows only the Focus's projects. The rest collapse into one quiet line at the bottom. The focus bar shows what you're in, until when, and your topic slots.

2 · Catching an idea without starting it

The urge is "build it right now". The catcher answers it with something that feels like progress but costs nothing: the idea is written down, filed in the right project, and you're back in your chat in two seconds.

Where you can jot from

⌥ Space anywhere on the Macglobal hotkey
The 💡 button in the focus barMac
Long-press the chat barphone / iPad
Dictation on the phone"idea for Nahar…"
Typed into the Desk boxrouter sees it's off-focus

Where it goes

Into that project's to-do list (already built), marked 💡 so ideas and real to-dos don't mix. The Desk's Haiku router guesses the project; you can fix it with one tap. If it's a brand-new project idea, it goes to an Inbox.

Optional: Claude can add a one-line "what this might take" under the idea later, when the project is back in focus, never now.

3 · The topic limit

A topic is one chat, by default. That's dumb on purpose: no agent has to decide what counts as "the same thing", so nothing gets misread. The Desk router can still say "this sounds like your Testo chat, send it there?" before you open a new one.

You're at 3 of 3 topics

Starting "Snapshot tests for forms" would be a 4th thing in your head.

Park it as an idea Put "PR review" to sleep It's part of "Checklist crash" Go over, just this once
"Go over" is allowed. It's counted, and shows in the end-of-focus recap. No guilt, just a mirror.

What frees a slot

Chat closed or put to sleepfrees it
PR merged from that chatfrees it
Idle for 2h with nothing waitingasks: still on it?
Subagents, workers, background tasksdon't count

5 · Phone and iPad

The Mac decides; the phone follows. The Mac simply doesn't send asleep projects' chats, cards, pushes or Live Activities, so it works even on an old build and nothing leaks through a notification.

6 · Things to decide

  • Decided: when a Focus starts and an out-of-focus chat is still working, Lantern asks each time: "Nahar · Week view is still working. Let it finish quietly, or pause it?"
  • Can Claude in a focused chat touch an asleep project's files? Probably yes, Focus is about your attention, not a sandbox.
  • Friction for stepping out: a reason + a timer (shown here), or a plain button?
  • Topic = chat, or let the Desk router group chats into topics and you confirm?

Rough build order

  1. Mac: a Focus = a Desk + a lock; sidebar hides the rest; focus bar; manual on/off.
  2. Idea catcher: hotkey + 💡 to-dos per project + the "ideas waiting" sheet on switch.
  3. Schedule, pause/vacation, step-out timer.
  4. Mac filters what it sends the phone (chats, pushes, Live Activities) + digest.
  5. Topic slots and the "3 of 3" card.
  6. Maybe: follow macOS Focus.
