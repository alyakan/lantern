Your AI coding agents keep working while you step away. Lantern is the remote control for them: see what each one is doing, and approve, answer or steer it from your phone.

Lantern needs Lantern for Mac, the free, open-source app where your agents run, on the same Wi-Fi. The phone talks to your Mac directly, encrypted. There is no account, no server in between, and no tracking.

WHAT WAITS ON YOU
Home leads with what needs a decision: the command an agent wants to run, the file it wants to change, the question it is asking. Everything else waits quietly below.

APPROVE WITH A SWIPE
Swipe to allow, tap to deny. Open the full screen to read the whole command first. Answer an agent's questions by picking an option or writing your own.

STAY IN THE CONVERSATION
Read a chat as it happens, with code, tables and lists shown properly. Send a message, stop a turn, wake a sleeping chat, or start a new chat in one of your projects (it always starts on Ask, so nothing runs without your say-so).

NOTHING TO DO? GET AN IDEA
When nothing is waiting, Lantern suggests what to pick up next, from your recent chats.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account. No analytics. No ads. Conversations are shown while the app is open and are not stored on the phone. The pairing key stays in your phone's secure storage.

FREE, WITH AN OPTIONAL TIP
Everything is free. If Lantern helps you, you can leave a tip under Support development.
